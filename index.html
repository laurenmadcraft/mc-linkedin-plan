02

Who we target and what we offer them

Two campaigns. Account Reach ($25/day) gets seen by decision-makers at named accounts. Retargeting ($25/day) turns engaged visitors into booked consultations. Last time retargeting was too top-of-funnel and too small; keeping "be seen" and "book a call" separate fixes that.

05

Measurement

Pipedrive is the source of truth. LinkedIn's own numbers are directional.

Weekly execution dashboard

  • Spend against budget
  • Share of Tier 1 accounts reached
  • Engaged target companies (sent to BD)
  • Consultation inquiries from LinkedIn
  • Ads swapped and why

Monthly commercial report

  • Qualified meetings LinkedIn sourced or assisted
  • Sales-accepted opportunities and pipeline value, by offer
  • Cost per qualified meeting
  • What we're changing next month
Attribution: every lead keeps its first touch, assisted touches, landing page, campaign, self-reported source ("How did you hear about us?") and offer, so LinkedIn's role isn't lost when another channel gets the last click.
