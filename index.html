02

What we're going to run

Three messages, one per offer, shown first to cold decision-makers and then, for those who engage, a follow-up asking them to book a call.

Headline offer

Brand + website

Cold accounts see"Your business has evolved faster than your brand and website. We transform both together." Image + video
Warm visitors see"Thinking about your brand and website? Talk to us about both in one free consultation."
Offer 2

Enterprise websites

Cold accounts see"Has your website kept pace with your business?" Image + video
Warm visitors see"Book a free website consultation: see where your site stands and what to fix first."
Offer 3

Online visibility (search + AI)

Cold accounts see"Your buyers are asking ChatGPT who to shortlist. Is your company in the answer?" Image + video
Warm visitors see"Find out where you show up on Google and AI search. Book a free consultation."

Useful content, from Month 2

Three content-plan articles run as document ads people can read in the feed: why B2B websites stop generating opportunities, when a business has outgrown its brand, and how buyers find suppliers through AI search.

Proof, as soon as it's approved

Case studies go into the Retargeting ads, so warm visitors see real work before they book. Two are already on the site: Mincon (branding) and CAD Journey (website).

Sales follow-up

Each week Megan gets the list of target companies engaging with these ads, so SDRs can call them while they're warm.

Launch set: 6 Account Reach ads + 3 Retargeting ads. Weakest ads get swapped every 2 weeks; a fresh round of creative every 4 to 6 weeks.
